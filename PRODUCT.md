# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS (user-confirmed). Deployed from a GitHub repo to Hostinger static hosting — no build step, no backend.

## Users

Business owners and decision-makers with real operational businesses (Turkish and international — the site is bilingual EN/TR with a language toggle) who suspect AI could remove manual work from their operations but don't know where to start or whom to trust. They arrive evaluating whether this person can actually build and ship automations, not just talk about them.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal landing site for Asım, who designs, builds, and ships AI automations for real businesses. Success = a qualified visitor submits the inquiry form (Formspree) or books a conversation.

## Positioning

Not an agency, not a course-seller: one builder who takes real business processes and turns them into working AI automations that run in production. The site itself must feel like proof — restrained, precise, effective.

## Operating Context

Visitor flow: land → understand the offer in one viewport (split hero: headline left, inquiry form right) → submit inquiry. Language toggle EN/TR persists per visitor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page, static; form posts to Formspree (endpoint ID to be supplied by the user — use a clearly marked placeholder).
- Hosting: Hostinger static (GitHub deploy). No server-side code.
- Bilingual EN/TR via client-side toggle; all copy exists in both languages.
- Black background is a binding user constraint.

## Brand Commitments

- No personal name or brand name displayed on the site (user-confirmed: "sitede isme gerek yok"). Identity is carried by the work and voice, not a logotype.
- Background: black. Overall direction: sade ve gösterişsiz ama etkili — minimal, unshowy, effective.
- Layout reference (user-provided): split hero — large headline left, inquiry form card right ("by Crawford" agency reference).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no testimonials, client logos, case studies, or metrics were provided. Do not fabricate proof — no invented client counts, avatars, or review numbers. (The reference site's "700+ people" social-proof strip must NOT be copied as fake proof.)

## Product Principles

- The page is the portfolio: restraint and precision stand in for testimonials until real proof exists.
- One action: get the visitor to the inquiry form; nothing competes with it.
- Speak about real business outcomes, not AI hype.
- Everything must work with zero backend and zero build step.
